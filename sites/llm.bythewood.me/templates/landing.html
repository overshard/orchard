proxy
A proxy, not a library

Anything that already speaks chat completions needs a base url and a header, not a rewrite. Streams and tool calls pass straight through.

keys
A key per caller

Every call is attributed to the key that made it. Revoking one stops it on the next request.

log
Every call written down

Prompt, completion, tokens and throughput are kept in full and aged out on a retention window. A call marked incognito writes no row at all.

card
Loaded on demand

The weights load on the first call and come off after three idle minutes. A desktop that is also a workstation gets its card back.

pictures
Chat and pictures take turns

A chat model and an image model share the one card, and whichever is asked for swaps in. The prompt is logged and the picture is not.

manners
Knows when the card is busy

A caller can ask how loaded the card is before it asks for anything. A background job uses that to wait rather than take it from somebody.
