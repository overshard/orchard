Using it

API

Send the key as a bearer token to /api/jobs. A job is a name, a kind of notify, prompt or quote, an at or a cron, and a message. A time with no offset is Eastern.

curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $CRON_KEY" https://cron.bythewood.me/api/jobs \
  -d '{"name": "Recycling", "cron": "0 19 * * 2", "message": "Take the recycling out"}'
