Using it

Send the key as Authorization: Bearer cron-… to https://cron.bythewood.me/api/jobs. A job is {"name", "kind", "at" or "cron", "message"}, kind is notify or prompt, and a time with no offset is Eastern.

curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $CRON_KEY" https://cron.bythewood.me/api/jobs \
  -d '{"name": "Switch 2", "at": "2026-11-22T08:00", "message": "Bundle is $499.99 today"}'
