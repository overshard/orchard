collection
One-line site tag

Drop a small async script in your <head>. It posts page views, clicks, scrolls, sessions, and custom events to one endpoint.

attribution
UTM + referrer tracking

Slice traffic by source, medium, and campaign. Referrers, pages, and custom events rank with period-over-period deltas.

geo + device
Maps, browsers, platforms

A Natural Earth world map with admin-1 drill-down, plus device, browser, and platform breakdowns. GeoIP runs locally so addresses stay on your host.

reports
Dashboard + PDF export

Flip any date range to PDF or markdown in one click, rendered in process by Typst. Custom cards pin whatever metric you want.

schema
Typed schema, JSON extras

Hot fields live in typed columns for fast queries, and custom keys land in an extra JSON column. Bot traffic routes to its own table at write time.

stack
One Go binary and a file

Go and SQLite, shipped as one small Alpine image behind your own reverse proxy. Your domain, your retention policy.
