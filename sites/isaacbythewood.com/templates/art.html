Acrylic Pours

A bit more traditional than my usual art. Each piece starts as a mix of acrylic paint, water, glue, and silicone oil poured onto canvas, then manipulated with a heat gun to bring out cells and organic patterns. No two pours come out the same and the process is as much about letting go of control as it is about technique.
