          Jobs

          Every frog past the egg can work one job. Work piles up for 12 hours and then waits for you. Levels go to 99 and only go up, a better frog brings in more, and a frog gets a quarter more at the job its nature suits. Rarer finds turn up at higher levels. Crafting turns sticks, reeds and clay into things, and a frog that's full from a dish works harder and learns faster. A gardener also makes the plots grow faster. A 99 earns the pond that job's hood, and once in a long while a frog brings home a pet.

          Egg bench

          An egg is its parent with the numbers nudged, and about one in sixteen throws back to something else. A dye, a stamp or eye drops take about two times in three and are used up either way. A frog fed a dish first does better, the rarer the dish the more often they take, and a rare one or better can give odd eyes on its own. Drops make a frog ten times rarer. Moth in town sells all three.

                      Neighbours

                      The neighbours are frogs who live round the pond, run by the pond itself and the same for everybody. Bring each one a dish a day and it gets friendlier, three times as fast with its favourite, and it gives you something at every heart. At two hearts it tells you how to cook its favourite. One of them asks a small favour each day, and ignoring it costs nothing.

                      Put up to eight things on your stall for a day. Neighbours wander by all day and somebody nearly always buys within the hour at the going rate, and the chance drops fast as the price goes up. What a thing goes for changes with the day and falls as more of it is put up. Asking over the rate costs a small fee that isn't given back. A hat or trinket has to be a spare, and whoever buys likes you a little more for it.
