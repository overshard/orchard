The Cloudflare custom rules, kept here because a dashboard rule is not in
version control and does not come back on a rebuild. Nothing reads this file.

Every rule is action Block. Rule 1 is pasted identically into the bythewood.me
and isaacbythewood.com zones, and a clause landing in one zone and not the other
has happened three times, so probe both after an edit. Rule 2 is bythewood.me
only. The free plan allows five per zone and caps each at 4,096 characters, and
the zone also holds an auth login origins block that isn't recorded here yet.

Cloudflare only takes the high volume families, so they never cross the tunnel.
Caddy's scanners snippets are the full list and catch the rest with a 404, so a
rare pattern goes there and not here. The free plan has no regex operator, so
rule 1 guards on the repository content shape with `contains "/blob/"` where the
Caddyfile anchors the verb to the second segment.

Rule 2, "repos history signed out", bythewood.me only:

  (http.host eq "repos.bythewood.me"
   and not http.cookie contains "bw_session="
   and (lower(http.request.uri.path) contains "/commit/"
        or ends_with(lower(http.request.uri.path), "/log")
        or lower(http.request.uri.path) contains "/log/"))

repos already answers these with a 404 to anyone signed out, so this only stops
the residential proxy crawl at the edge. Commits and history at a SHA under
blob, raw and tree need regex to tell from a branch view, so they still reach
repos.
