The four Cloudflare custom rules, kept here because a dashboard rule is not in
version control and does not come back on a rebuild. Nothing reads this file.

All four are action Block, pasted identically into the bythewood.me and
isaacbythewood.com zones. Four rather than one, of the five the free plan
allows, since together they exceed the 4,096 character cap on a single
expression. A clause landing in one zone and not the other has happened three
times, so probe both after an edit.

Cloudflare is the loose layer and Caddy the exact one. The free plan has no
regex operator, so rule 1 guards on the repository content shape with
`contains "/blob/"` where the Caddyfile anchors the verb to the second segment.
Rule 2 guards on the host instead, because the families in it never collide with
a file in a repository and Caddy covers repos exactly anyway.
